About

I studied psychology to understand how people think and decide. Then I learned to build software so I could stop handing my ideas to someone else. Today I combine both: leading AI products that help real people make real decisions.

At Frontnow I head the Experience department and lead two enterprise AI products with the team: Advise, a conversational AI sales assistant live across European retail, and Enhance, AI-powered product data enrichment for retailers.

How I work

  • Think in systems. Products live in systems of people, data and incentives. I design for the whole, not the feature.
  • Measure what matters. Shipping is not the finish line. I define what good looks like before we build, then hold the product to it.
  • Design for humans. AI is only useful when people trust it. I start with the human, not the model.
