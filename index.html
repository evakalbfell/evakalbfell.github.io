How I work

  • Think in systems, not features. Intelligent products are living systems. Every decision creates a feedback loop, and structure determines intelligence.
  • Measure what matters. Data is not proof; it’s conversation. Everything is an iteration. Metrics exist to guide learning, not to declare success.
  • Design for humans. AI systems exist to serve human understanding, not replace it. The goal is to turn complexity into experiences that empower.
